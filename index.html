Howdy! I am a computational linguist interested in discourse and semantics. My work focuses on using LMs to better understand about how people use language.

I am currently a PhD student at Georgetown University, advised by Amir Zeldes. Prior to that, I earned my BA and BS in Linguistics and Computer Science respectively from UT Austin, where I worked with Jessy Li and Kanishka Misra.

When I'm not thinking about lanugage, I like to play the electric bass, go out into nature, and read books. My favorite national parks are Death Valley and Joshua Tree, and my favorite book is Frankenstein by Mary Shelley. I'm also a big fan of Emoji 🌃
